# Theme Summary

- Background: warm off-white, primarily `#faf8f5` / `#f8f5f1`
- Surface: `#ffffff`
- Ink: charcoal `#403a35`
- Muted text: `#918981`
- Border: `#ebe5df`
- Accent: orange `#e96d32`
- Accent soft: `#fff0e6`
- Radius: 8px controls, 12-14px cards
- Shadow: subtle `rgba(70,52,37,.035)` card shadow
- Typography: compact sans-serif, tight headings, small utility labels
- Breakpoints: 1100px, 800px, 600px, 520px

The page styles are in `client/src/App.css` and global tokens are in `client/src/index.css`.
